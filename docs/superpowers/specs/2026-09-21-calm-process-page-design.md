# CALM Process and Research Page Design

## Scope

Add one new CALM page immediately after the two existing CALM pages. The existing CALM pages remain unchanged. The portfolio increases from 15 to 16 pages; all following pages shift forward by one position.

## Purpose

The new page must prove that CALM is an authored research and development system, not only a polished visual outcome. It should let a creative-technology hiring manager understand the chain from physical investigation to structured analysis, experimentation and responsive behaviour in under twenty seconds.

## Narrative

Headline: **CALM / ONE PAINTING, SEVENTY REGIONS, A LIVING MATERIAL.**

Reading order:

1. Research premise and source decomposition.
2. Seventy-region authored analysis.
3. Behavioural control maps.
4. Material iteration and rejected directions.
5. Live causal behaviour and retained state.

The page reads left to right as:

**research -> analysis -> controls -> iteration -> behaviour**

## Composition

Use the established warm research-folio language from the approved CALM material-system page:

- 16:9 landscape page.
- Warm paper ground with subtle texture.
- Rounded outer folio frame.
- Black, warm-grey and restrained coral typography.
- Condensed display headline, monospaced evidence labels and quiet sans-serif annotations.
- Vector typography, rules, labels, numbering and diagram connectors.
- Raster imagery only for evidence photographs and authored research boards.
- Generous margins and clear reading order; no screenshot collage or dense evidence wall.

The main field contains five evidence zones:

1. **Research premise** - a distinct physical-study or forensic board not present on either existing CALM page.
2. **70-region analysis** - the numbered-region system and/or complete authored system map.
3. **Control logic** - a selective strip of membrane, tear and roughness controls. Do not reuse the height, fibre or normal images already represented on the approved material-system page.
4. **Iteration lineage** - selected V1-to-V85 stages including one clearly labelled rejected direction and one resolved material state.
5. **Live behaviour** - causal prototype evidence showing input, state transition and retained memory.

## Image Non-Repetition Rule

Do not repeat or recrop any image already visible on the two existing CALM pages. Explicitly exclude:

- The CALM cover/hero material image.
- The original painting used on the material-system page.
- The silver surface-study image.
- The monochrome fibre-direction image.
- The WebGL material-interface frame.

Use only distinct evidence from the CALM source archive and source-package boards. Repetition is evaluated visually, not only by filename.

## Copy Structure

Keep copy concise and evidence-led:

- One two-sentence framing paragraph.
- Five numbered evidence labels.
- One short caption per evidence zone.
- One concluding line linking authored analysis to live behaviour.

Avoid invented performance metrics or claims unsupported by the project archive.

## Quality Requirements

- All typography and rules remain vector-sharp at any zoom.
- Every raster asset must have sufficient source resolution for at least 250 effective PPI at placed size; aim for 300 PPI or higher.
- No image may be stretched, distorted or cropped so tightly that its subject becomes unclear.
- No caption may be smaller than the established CALM folio captions.
- The page must remain readable as a full-page PDF view and reward closer inspection.
- The other 15 pages must render identically after insertion.

## Verification

Before delivery:

1. Render and inspect the new page at full size.
2. Verify every selected image is distinct from the two existing CALM pages.
3. Inspect embedded-image effective resolution.
4. Confirm the final PDF has 16 pages.
5. Pixel-compare the original 15 pages against their shifted counterparts; only the inserted page may be new.
6. Verify the deployed file matches the local final PDF byte-for-byte.
